# Independent Rapitec / NSAS DA review, 2026-11-16

An independent GPT-6 Astra analysis examined the supplied binary pair read-only.
This note records evidence and unresolved interpretation, not vendor certification.
The follow-up implementation review found two documentation overstatements,
both corrected: the DA header's 2500 has unverified exclusive/nominal-boundary
semantics, and each record is a stored spectrum with 32 acquisition scans rather
than an individual stored scan. No actionable decoder defect was reported.

## Confirmed beyond the spectral payload

All 75 SPC subheaders mirror the DA vendor record number `n` as `subindx=n-1`,
`subtime=float(n-1)` and `subnext=float(n)`. Their `subscan` is 32. Thus SPC's
subtime is an ordinal-like export field here, not an independently measured time.

The SPC declared count 7 equals both the highest and the final DA vendor record
number. Its log lists exactly the first seven physical records. Confusing that
local number with a global count is a plausible exporter failure, but these files
cannot distinguish a final-index rule from a maximum-index rule or another cause.

No two stored spectra are identical. Both sample ID and record number must be
preserved alongside the physical ordinal: one sample has two different
number-2 acquisitions at physical zero-based indices 44 and 45, approximately
nine minutes apart. Series also contain skipped and shifted numbers.

The file is exactly 75 × 17 × 256 bytes, with no separate global header,
directory, residual tail or non-space trailer in this example. All bytes belong
to the documented record regions. This establishes byte coverage; it does not
give semantic meaning to every constant header word.

## Additional observations, without inferred chemistry

Sensors 1 and 2 are exact float32 multiples of approximately
`5.72213320992887e-6`, compatible with quantized telemetry. Their physical units
and role are unidentified. Sensor 3 shows a measurable excursion over five acquisitions of one sample.
Neither a temperature interpretation nor a concentration interpretation follows
from the bytes.

The strongest median spectral curvatures occur near 1098 and 1100 nm. This is
compatible with a detector junction, but no detector labels or hardware partition
are encoded explicitly in the recovered fields. The reader preserves the original
axis and values without splitting or correcting that feature.

## Remaining evidence needed

The constant words at offsets 20, 22, 52–62, 78 and 92 cannot be fully identified
from one file. Other spectral ranges, instrument models, scan counts or measurement
types would allow controlled comparisons. All unknown header bytes are retained.

No decoded constituent reference table, regression equation, operation settings
or explicit link to `.CA`, `.OPR` or `RAPITEC.RAM` is present. Ask for those files
with original names/directories, documentation, NSAS/Rapitec versions, a matching
NSAS text export containing constituents, and definitions of local numbers and
transducers. The existing sample IDs, timestamps and physical order are anchors
for investigating those relationships, not proof that a calibration can be rebuilt.
