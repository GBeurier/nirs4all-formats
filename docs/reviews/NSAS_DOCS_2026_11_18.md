# Rapitec / NSAS documentation review, 2026-11-18

The follow-up archive contains three scanned PDFs and two additional DA files,
but no CA, OPR or RAM calibration binary. The original archive and extracted
files are retained without modification under the ignored local corpus.

## Evidence handling

The PDFs total 128 pages. Their initial text extraction was empty, so local OCR
was used after per-page orientation detection. The first OCR pass contained
upside-down recognition errors; it is retained locally for audit and is not used
as evidence. The original scans remain authoritative. Important file-role,
filename and equation passages were visually checked.

The NSAS chapter has an unusual ordering: the first PDF holds the 31 odd pages,
while the second holds the 31 even pages in reverse order. The local reading-order
copy restores printed pages 3-1 through 3-62 without changing either source PDF.

An independent Astra review checked the calibration chapter and the integrated
workflow analysis. It confirmed the page references and the factory calibration
filenames and reported no factual correction to the final analysis.

## Conclusions qualified for integration

- File roles, calibration-development context, product ordering and instrument
  deployment are documented in `docs/formats/foss-nsas-workflow.md`, with exact
  PDF and printed-page citations.
- Transducer values can be regression inputs. Their individual units and their
  actual use in these acquisitions remain unknown.
- NSAS can associate laboratory information with data files. That is not evidence
  of a decoded reference-value field in the received DA examples.
- No new numeric meaning is assigned to constant DA header words. No calibration
  binary layout or recovered coefficients are claimed.
- The two new DA files contain 60 and 56 records. Alongside the first file, all
  191 records / 200550 spectral values pass exact full-file reconstruction and
  path/bytes parity. Only the first file has a paired SPC reference export.

The runtime change updates one warning to distinguish the documented logical
workflow from unverified calibration byte layouts. The reviewed golden change
is limited to that text in the three existing synthetic records; arrays,
metadata and fixture hashes remain unchanged.
